We’re living through what the World Economic Forum calls the Fourth Industrial Revolution – a wave of AI, robotics, and digitalization that is “fundamentally changing the way we live, work and relate to one another.” Like all previous industrial revolutions this one isn’t just transforming human life it’s reshaping the Earth system itself. Rising global temperatures, accelerating ice loss, sea-level rise, more frequent wildfires, shifting weather patterns, and biodiversity loss are all linked to human-driven greenhouse gas emissions. AI is now a visible part of that story.
