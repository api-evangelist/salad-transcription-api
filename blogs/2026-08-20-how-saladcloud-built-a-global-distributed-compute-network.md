Distributed computing makes it possible to build global infrastructure in a very different way. Instead of opening data centers country by country, SaladCloud grew by connecting compute that was already there – gaming PCs and workstations owned by people around the world. Today, that network spans 191 countries, with hundreds of thousands of machines contributing resources.
